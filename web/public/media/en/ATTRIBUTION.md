# Audio attribution

The English demo meeting shown in the English cut of the promo video was produced by running
mm2t's own pipeline on an excerpt of meeting **ES2002a** (13:00–21:12 of `ES2002a.Mix-Headset.wav`)
from the **AMI Meeting Corpus**.

- Source: AMI Meeting Corpus, University of Edinburgh and the AMI Consortium — https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Changes: trimmed to an 8-minute excerpt and resampled to 48 kHz. The audio itself is not part of the video;
  only the app's transcript, summary and notes derived from it appear on screen.
- The speaker names shown (Olivia Hart, Ben Carter, Daniel Brooks, Priya Nair, Sam Ortiz) are invented
  for the demo and are not the corpus participants' names.

The Chinese cut uses a Mandarin meeting from the project's own benchmark set; its speaker names are also invented.

The English website App captures in `app/` are still frames extracted from the same demo recordings. They retain the original App interface and derived meeting text, with resizing and WebP compression only.
