# Refund Policy

Revision date: 2026-11-23

This policy describes how mm2t handles transaction inquiries, subscription cancellation, refunds, and consumer remedies for its paid cloud services. A “receipt” is the order confirmation or proof of payment issued for a completed transaction.

## 1. Covered transactions {#transactions}

**Paid mm2t cloud orders.** This policy covers first purchases, renewals, and payment disputes involving paid plans. Use the information on your receipt to identify the charge.

## 2. Cancelling renewal {#cancellation}

**How to cancel.** Use the cancellation option on the receipt, subscription confirmation, or billing page. Check the effective date and current service period displayed when cancellation is confirmed.

**Cancellation and refunds.** Cancelling renewal stops future recurring charges. To request a refund or exercise a statutory withdrawal right, use the relevant request process and keep confirmation.

## 3. Reasons to ask for a review {#request-reasons}

You can ask for a review of duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, paid services that were not delivered, defects, charges after a confirmed cancellation date, or charges that otherwise differ from your order. Describe what happened and the outcome you are seeking. For an unauthorized transaction, you can also ask your payment issuer about protective and dispute measures.

## 4. How to submit a request {#requesting-a-refund}

**Contact and requests.** Email [oscar48079@gmail.com](mailto:oscar48079@gmail.com) and say whether you are requesting a refund, asking about a charge, or exercising a statutory withdrawal right. You may also use the payment support channel listed on your receipt, subscription confirmation, or billing page.

**Order details.** Provide the minimum information needed to identify the order, such as the order number, purchase email, charge date, and amount, along with a brief reason. Keep full card numbers, banking passwords, verification codes, and meeting content out of the request. Keep transaction details out of public comments.

**Paddle orders.** For receipts that identify Paddle as the entity handling the order, use the management link on the receipt or [Paddle payment support](https://paddle.net) for refunds and cancellation. See the [Paddle Refund Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) and [Paddle buyer support](https://www.paddle.com/help/manage/your-customers/buyers-refunds) for those channels.

## 5. Review and payment of refunds {#refund-processing}

**Review.** Payment support checks the order and the circumstances you describe, handles the request under the transaction terms and consumer rights, and communicates the outcome or asks for information it needs through a private channel.

**Refund method.** After approval, the payment service handles the refund according to the original payment method and the financial institution’s rules. Ask payment support to confirm the refund method and posting status.

## 6. Refunds and plan access {#plan-access}

A refund and a subscription change are separate events. An approved refund may change the plan, paid features, and cloud allowance associated with that transaction. See the refund confirmation and billing page for the effects and dates shown. You can export meeting data using available features.

## 7. Statutory withdrawal and remedies {#consumer-rights}

You have the withdrawal, refund, and other remedies that applicable law provides for distance transactions, non-delivery, defective services, and improper charges. Consumers covered by Taiwan’s distance-transaction rules can give written notice to exercise their withdrawal right within the statutory period. Keep proof of when you sent the notice.

Taiwan law sets separate requirements for certain digital content and online services completed upon delivery. You have the right to receive the required disclosures and make an informed choice about advance consent. See [Article 19 of Taiwan’s Consumer Protection Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170001&flno=19) and [Article 2 of the applicable regulations](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawSingle.aspx?pcode=J0170012&flno=2).
