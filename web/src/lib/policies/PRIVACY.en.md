# Privacy Policy

Revision date: 2026-11-23

This policy describes how we handle personal data when you use the cloud services operated by mm2t, including accounts, plans, subscriptions, and related cloud features (together, the “Service”). “We” refers to the Service operator.

## 1. Account, usage, and transaction records {#account-and-billing}

**Account data.** For account features, we process your account ID, email address, name, sign-in provider identifier, and sign-in session. When you use Google or Apple to sign in, the provider receives the sign-in request and returns the authorized information needed to create or verify your account.

**Plan and usage.** Usage records can include your plan, period, transcription time, AI usage, processing sessions, and request status. They are used to provide features, calculate quotas, handle errors, and prevent abuse.

**Payment records.** When you buy a paid plan, the payment service for that transaction handles payment information through checkout. mm2t can receive order, customer, and subscription identifiers, transaction events, and payment and refund status to activate plans, reconcile accounts, and support transactions. Payment data for orders handled by Paddle is processed under the [Paddle Privacy Policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).

## 2. Cloud processing and meeting data {#cloud-processing}

**Cloud content.** Your client can send data to the Service when you use a cloud feature. Depending on the feature, this can include microphone or remote audio tracks, audio segments, language and vocabulary hints, transcripts, summaries, assistant prompts and needed conversation context, titles, speaker labels, timestamps, and other meeting metadata. The Service uses the data for transcription, captions, summaries or answers, meeting storage or collaboration you enable, and necessary usage accounting. We may keep request identifiers, audio identifiers, or segment transcription results to support retries and usage accounting.

**Live captions.** Enabling cloud live captions sends the audio needed for that feature to the provider delivering the captions.

## 3. Why we process data {#app-limitations}

Data is processed to provide selected features, verify identity and access, protect the service, handle subscriptions and transactions, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations.

## 4. Collaboration, sharing, and recipients {#sharing}

When collaboration is used, the service processes the room or meeting title, sharing identifiers, collaboration documents, and components you share. Participants with access or people holding an active share link can view the shared content and act according to their permissions. When caption-based AI suggestions are enabled, the selected LLM may process recent captions before you start sharing to decide whether to suggest a collaboration component.

Choose recipients based on the sensitivity of your content. Recipients may save copies or forward the link. Ending sharing or disabling a link can affect future access. Recipients manage copies they have already saved.

## 5. Who receives data {#providers-and-transfers}

Recipients may include hosting and storage services, sign-in providers, speech recognition and LLM inference providers, payment services, and recipients you select for sharing, depending on the features and options you use. Inference providers can receive the data needed for transcription, captions, or text processing.

## 6. Retention, deletion, and backups {#deleting-data}

Cloud retention depends on the category and purpose, such as transient processing data, meetings you choose to store, collaboration documents, logs, and accounting records. You can request deletion of cloud data using the email address in this policy. After cloud deletion, backup copies may persist until the backup cycle rotates them out. Transaction records may be retained as needed for tax, accounting, legal retention, or dispute handling.

## 7. Privacy rights and requests {#rights}

Under applicable law, you may ask us to provide access to or a copy of your personal data, correct it, stop collecting, processing, or using it, or delete it. You may also be able to complain to the competent authority where you live. The recipient of your request may ask for proportionate proof of identity or authority. Send privacy rights requests to the email address in this policy.

## 8. Data security {#security}

We apply safeguards appropriate to the type of data and risk.  Account authentication and sharing permissions control access to cloud features and shared content.

**Security incidents.** We may use incident-related connection, service request, account authentication, usage, and incident records to identify, investigate, and address suspected abuse or security incidents. We limit the use and retention of these records to what we need to investigate and address the incident and meet legal obligations.

## 9. International processing {#international-transfers}

Our cloud services and providers may process data outside your location. International processing follows applicable privacy and data transfer rules.

## 10. Recording and other participants {#recording-and-participants}

Recordings or transcripts submitted to the cloud service may contain other participants’ data. Where required by law, meeting rules, or confidentiality obligations, give notice and obtain consent or authorization before recording, uploading, or sharing. Check your intended recipients before sharing.

## 11. Policy changes {#changes}

We identify revisions to this policy. We give notice or obtain consent as required by law for changes to data uses, recipients, or retention.
